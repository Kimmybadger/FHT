🙌 Handwashing Steps · 손 씻기 순서
  • 1
    Wet hands with warm running water
    따뜻한 흐르는 물로 손을 적신다
  • 2
    Apply soap and lather
    비누를 묻혀 거품을 낸다
  • 3
    Scrub ≥ 20 seconds — fingers, thumbs, between fingers, backs
    20초 이상 문지르기 — 손가락, 엄지, 손가락 사이, 손등
  • 4
    Rinse thoroughly under running water
    흐르는 물에 깨끗이 헹군다
  • 5
    Dry with single-use paper towel
    1회용 종이 타월로 건조
  • 6
    Use towel to turn off faucet
    타월로 수도꼭지를 잠근다

Food Operation Safety Course

Follow the instructions below to receive the Food Handler Certification:

  1. Complete all 5 training modules in the Learn tab.
  2. Your first and last name are required to print your certification card.
  3. Score 80% or higher on the quiz (20 out of 25 questions).
  4. After passing, generate your certificate and save as PDF immediately.
  5. Your certificate will be emailed to EH — bring it to food handler events.
Privacy Advisory By completing this course, you certify that you are the person taking the training and entitled to the certification card. Your first and last name will be printed on your certification card. Certificates expire one (1) year from the date of issue. If your certificate has expired, complete the training and pass the quiz again to receive a new certificate.
⚠️ The certificate is issued only once per session. Save your PDF before closing — it cannot be reissued after the window is closed.
NOTE: This course is intended for person(s) conducting ORGANIZATIONAL FOOD EVENTS open to the general public on the installation. Basic Food Handler certification DOES NOT fulfill the requirements of 4-hour initial/annual food safety training required of FOOD EMPLOYEES working in a FOOD ESTABLISHMENT.
